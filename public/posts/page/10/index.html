RANE TWELVE MKII Software & Gear Specs Breakdown

RANE TWELVE MKII Specs & Compatibility

The RANE TWELVE MKII is a 1-channel DJ setup built for performance.

  • Brand: RANE
  • Model: TWELVE MKII
